\texttt{NormalizerSideCertificate} records properties of the actual
linear map \texttt{closedWord}, rather than checks at sampled lengths.
Its proof-bearing fields establish:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item injectivity of the coefficient-to-vector map and a nonzero Gram entry;
\item the restricted symplectic-product formula and the radical condition;
\item normalizer dimension $q+2$ and radical-coefficient dimension $q$;
\item weight at least $n-1$ outside the radical and a vector attaining $n-1$.
\end{itemize}
The injective parametrization identifies the coefficient radical with
the radical of the constructed space. The standard stabilizer
correspondence in Appendix~\ref{app:symplectic} then gives
$k=1$, $d=n-1$ and $c=n-q-1$. The certificate also stores a parameter
record and BDH excess $q-2$ as default data fields; these fields are not
additional proofs of the correspondence. Lean checks the algebraic
certificate; the paper supplies its coding-theoretic interpretation.

\paragraph{Coordinate convention.}
The formalization indexes paired coordinates by $\mathbb{F}\times\mathrm{Bool}$
and puts the distinguished $\lambda$ coordinate first in the tail.
The displayed construction in Appendix~\ref{app:theorem} puts it last.
These are related by a coordinate permutation, which preserves the
symplectic product and weight. The declaration's existential $z_2$ is
instantiated by the proof as $0$ in characteristic two and $1$ otherwise.
