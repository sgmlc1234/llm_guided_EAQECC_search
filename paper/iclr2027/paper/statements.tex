\subsection*{AI use statement}
We used generative AI in the research workflow and in preparing this
paper. The discovery campaigns used \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash} and
\texttt{gemini-3.1-pro-preview} through AlphaEvolve; the controlled
study used Gemini 3.5 Flash with a local controller. These models
proposed and revised executable search programs in response to
researcher-supplied directions and exact evaluation feedback
(Sections~\ref{sec:pipeline} and~\ref{sec:ablation}). LLM-based coding
assistants, including Codex, also supported hypothesis and methodological refinement,
experiment design and calibration, implementation of search, analysis,
verification and certificate-generation tools, qualitative review of
program changes, and interpretation of results. Assistance with mathematical claims and proof development
included the coordinate-normalization argument in
Lemma~\ref{lem:coordinate-normalization}. Additional uses included
data normalization and reformatting, literature search and synthesis,
English drafting, translation and language editing of research discussions,
manuscript restructuring and editing, and preparation of figures,
tables and reproducibility artifacts. Generated codes are
mathematical outputs whose parameters are independently checked.
Verification uses exact finite-field computations, independent
linear-algebra checks, Magma cross-checks and checked proof certificates
as detailed in Appendices~\ref{app:pipeline-detail}--\ref{app:ablations};
these computational checks do not mechanize every mathematical argument.
Researcher guidance and mathematical generalization remain part of the
disclosed workflow. The authors take responsibility for the final text,
mathematical claims, proofs, code and reported results, including all
AI-assisted contributions.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work studies algebraic code constructions and search programs;
it does not involve experiments on human participants or use
participant-level or personal datasets. Its potential benefit is to
improve methods for quantum error correction and communication, while
the reported idealized parameters do not establish hardware-level
reliability or operational security. Research-integrity considerations
are addressed through dated sources, explicit provenance, independent
verification, and separation of new parameter claims from retrospective
reconstruction. Unsuccessful searches are distinguished from proved
nonexistence. The controlled-study records preserve failed and excluded
attempts, unavailable responses and protocol amendments, and its reviewer
bundle omits account credentials and machine-local configuration.
Researcher and AI contributions are disclosed, external sources are
cited, and the artifact retains software licensing information. Compute
allowances and model usage are documented in Appendices~\ref{app:campaign}
and~\ref{app:ablations}.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
Appendix~\ref{app:domain} specifies the code representation, mathematical
assumptions, constructions and proofs, and the reference table data
used for comparisons. Appendix~\ref{app:pipeline-detail} gives campaign
configuration, preserved prompts, verification methods and refutation
encodings, including the scope of certificate checks.
Section~\ref{sec:repro} and Appendix~\ref{app:repro} describe the auditor
and its verification scope; the artifact README gives installation,
integrity checks and fixed-program replay commands. Appendix~\ref{app:ablations} and the accompanying
\texttt{experiments/hitl\_ablation/} bundle preserve calibration and
evaluation seeds, budgets, attempted requests, received program sources,
parent relationships, selection records, interruption amendments and
independently checked codes. The artifact supplies offline audit
and replay entry points and is released under the MIT license.
These records support recomputation of reported quantities and replay
of preserved programs; fresh LLM sampling is not guaranteed to reproduce
the same generated programs. External solver and Magma availability
is reported separately from completed checks.
